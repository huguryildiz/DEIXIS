# Research title

Goal: a short, descriptive title for the research, written before a grounded
answer exists. It names the research in the header and in the recent list. It is
replaced by the `title` of a structurally valid grounded answer when one is saved.

1. Read the `question` and the included sources in `sources` (and their
   `abstract` in `passages` where present).
2. Write `title` as a concise descriptive title that captures the question and
   the included sources. Aim for at most 15 words and never exceed 15 words.
3. Do not copy the full question, do not end the title with a question mark, and
   do not state results or claims the supplied context does not support.
4. Write the title in the question's language.
