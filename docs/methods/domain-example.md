# User-supplied domain example

The following is an example of the extraction detail expected for one topic.
It is not the application's fixed scope and is not a request to execute this
literature review during consolidation. The field baseline should distinguish
foundational influence, bibliometric prominence and direct relevance. Fields that
do not apply to a study remain not applicable, with a reason.

## Task 1 — Establish the field baseline

Identify:

1. The foundational or most highly cited paper in molecular communication.
2. The most important review on molecular communication and IoBNT.
3. The closest primary studies on algae, photosynthetic organisms, biological signalling, or algae-related communication.
4. The closest existing OR studies involving molecular communication, IoBNT, biological networks, WSN/UWSN, resource allocation, scheduling, assignment, network design, or lifetime optimization.

For every paper report:

- complete citation,
- DOI,
- publication year,
- citation-count source or bibliometric proxy,
- reason for its importance,
- system and communication mechanism,
- mathematical formulation,
- demonstrated findings,
- limitations,
- proposed future work,
- exact PDF page and section for every important limitation.

Do not treat “most famous paper” as an objective fact. Explain whether the selection
is based on citation count, foundational influence, review frequency, or direct
relevance to algae.

## Task 2 — Construct an evidence matrix

Create a table with these columns:

- Paper
- Biological system or species
- Communication modality
- Signalling molecule or physical carrier
- Transmitter mechanism
- Propagation/channel model
- Receiver/detection mechanism
- Controllable variables
- Measured parameters
- Unmeasured assumptions
- Environmental conditions
- Spatial scale
- Temporal scale
- Demonstrated result
- Limitation
- Proposed future work
- Exact page/section/equation
- Relevance to algae-specific OR
- Evidence status: DEMONSTRATED / MODELLED / PROPOSED / INFERRED

## Generalization discussed

Preserve this level of specificity while adapting the columns to the user's domain.
Keep claim-level evidence in a long table behind the wide paper-comparison view.
Record reading depth independently. Missing full text cannot produce verified PDF
page claims. Method applicability, prior-work overlap and contribution value must
not collapse into a single score. A simulation is not biological demonstration.
