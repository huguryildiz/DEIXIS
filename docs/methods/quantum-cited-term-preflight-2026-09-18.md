# Cited-paper term lane preflight refinement

This addendum was written before any Gemini or OpenAlex call for the cited-paper lane. The earlier auxiliary protocol selected the three most-cited papers whose titles contained `entanglement` plus a broad topic word. An offline preflight on the frozen C pool showed that this selected foundational state-transfer, satellite-demonstration, and multipartite-teleportation papers (2,241, 1,473, and 567 OpenAlex citations), rather than papers focused on optimization or routing models. This is a selection-rule failure visible before term generation, not a result-driven rescue.

For this one-round diagnostic, require the title to contain `entanglement` plus `optimization`, `routing`, `resource allocation`, or `scheduling`, with a nonempty abstract and no `survey`, `review`, or `taxonomy` title word. Retain descending citation count and distinct normalized titles. The source remains the frozen `c_feature_guard` candidate pool, with three seeds, one Gemini medium proposal, one validated OpenAlex query, two pages, and no recursive learning. Record this refinement and both candidate rankings. Citation counts still do not establish relevance or quality. The survey lane's already-frozen protocol and run remain untouched.
